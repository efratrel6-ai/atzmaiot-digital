# team-builder: a skill for building a personal AI team

This folder holds the skill `team-builder/`. It sets up a personal AI team for a small business.
- The skill interviews the business owner in Hebrew, one question at a time.
- It then builds her a private "הצוות שלי" page: a chat with each member, a team group, a task board and a strategy meeting.

How to share it with a friend (a Hebrew text you can paste is in the chat):
1. Send her the file `team-builder.zip`.
2. She uploads it in Claude: Settings > Capabilities > Skills > Upload skill.
3. She writes to Claude: "תבני לי צוות אישי". The interview starts by itself.

It contains no data from Efrat's business. Each friend builds her own copy, and her information stays only in her private area.
